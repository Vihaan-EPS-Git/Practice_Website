Math - Algebra 1

Course Syllabus & Learning Methods

The Algebra 1 curriculum is designed to build a rock-solid foundation by covering essential core topics through interactive step-by-step conceptual breakdowns and real-world problem-solving exercises.

Specific Units & Things to Be Learned:

  • Unit 1: Foundations of Algebra (variables, order of operations, and expressions)
  • Unit 2: Solving Linear Equations & Inequalities in One Variable
  • Unit 3: Working with Functions, Domain, Range, and Function Notation
  • Unit 4: Linear Functions, Slopes, Intercepts, and Graphing Lines
  • Unit 5: Systems of Linear Equations & Inequalities
  • Unit 6: Exponents, Polynomial Operations, and Factoring Quadratics

Algebra 1 Overview

Algebra 1 builds the foundational language of higher mathematics by teaching students how to translate real-world scenarios into algebraic equations, manipulate variables, and solve multi-step problems with confidence. Through this course, students will master linear functions, analyze systems of equations, graph inequalities, and build essential problem-solving skills that serve as the critical gateway to geometry, advanced algebra, calculus, and STEM fields.

Learning Methods

Our Algebra 1 course employs a blended learning approach that combines interactive video lessons, guided practice problems, and real-world applications. Students will engage in hands-on activities, collaborative projects, and self-paced exercises to reinforce their understanding of algebraic concepts. Regular assessments and feedback ensure that students are mastering the material and building confidence in their mathematical abilities.
