Language - Spanish 3

Course Syllabus & Learning Methods

The Spanish 3 curriculum develops advanced communication and fluency through authentic literature, cultural studies, and complex grammatical constructs. Students master the subjunctive mood, conditional tenses, and nuanced phrasing to express hypotheses, opinions, and abstract ideas.

Specific Units & Things to Be Learned:

  • Unit 1: Art, Music, Theatre, and Cultural Expression in the Spanish-Speaking World
  • Unit 2: Careers, Workplaces, Future Aspirations, and the Future/Conditional Tenses
  • Unit 3: Environmental Issues, Nature, Conservation, and the Subjunctive in Adjective Clauses
  • Unit 4: Technology, Science Innovations, and Advanced Hypotheses
  • Unit 5: Literature, Short Stories, Poetry, and Narrative Analysis
  • Unit 6: Global Citizenship, Social Issues, and Advanced Subjunctive Mood Usage

Spanish 3 Overview

Develop advanced communication skills in Spanish through literary analysis, complex grammar, and immersive cultural studies.

Learning Methods

Our Spanish 3 course emphasizes text analysis, creative essay composition, and advanced subjunctive and conditional discussions.
