Language - Spanish 2

Course Syllabus & Learning Methods

The Spanish 2 curriculum builds upon foundational language skills by introducing more complex grammar structures, expanded vocabulary, past tense narratives, and nuanced conversational contexts. Students enhance their fluency and comprehension through structured writing and interactive dialogue exercises.

Specific Units & Things to Be Learned:

  • Unit 1: Daily Routines, Reflexive Verbs, and Health/Wellness
  • Unit 2: Shopping, Clothing, Markets, and Past Tense (Preterite of Regular Verbs)
  • Unit 3: Childhood Memories, Descriptions, and Past Tense (Imperfect vs. Preterite)
  • Unit 4: Travel, Transportation, Directions, and Vacation Planning
  • Unit 5: Emergencies, Health Accidents, and Hospital Vocabulary
  • Unit 6: House, Chores, Technology, and Cultural Celebrations Across Latin America

Spanish 2 Overview

Build upon your foundation in Spanish with more complex past-tense grammar, expanded vocabulary, and narrative storytelling.

Learning Methods

Our Spanish 2 course emphasizes conversational past-tense practice, guided composition, and contextual cultural analysis.
